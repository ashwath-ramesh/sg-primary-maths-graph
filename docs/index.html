How Singapore primary maths topics build on each other

Every topic in MOE's 2021 Primary Mathematics syllabus, from Primary 1 to Primary 6, and the earlier topics each one builds on.

How to use it: click a topic your child finds hard. Solid lines trace back to what it builds on; dashed lines show what it leads to. The side panel lists the syllabus outcomes and why each link is there.

Source: topic names and learning outcomes are copied word for word from the MOE Primary Mathematics syllabus (2021 syllabus, updated October 2025). The links between topics are my own judgment; MOE doesn't publish them.
